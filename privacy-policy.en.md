## 2. What this app does

True Love is an entertainment app that lets you answer three multiple-choice questions and, based on your choices, builds a **fictional** romantic letter on your device.

- The app **does not** require an account, login, email, password, or payment.
- The app **is not** a dating service, does not perform matchmaking, and does not promise meetings, predictions, or outcomes about your life.
- The letter is assembled from pre-written fragments. The app **does not use generative AI** to create the text.
- The app is intended for people **aged 18 or over**.

## 4. Data that stays on your device

### 4.1 Locally stored data

The app stores the following in its private storage:

- the **name** you enter during onboarding;
- your **analytics preference** (enabled or disabled);
- the **record that you accepted the Terms of Use** and the accepted version;
- the **anonymous identifier** of the PostHog SDK (generated locally) and the **local event queue**, when analytics is enabled;
- technical preferences needed for the app to work.

This data **is not sent to Jama Tech**. It remains on the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in Android settings.

The local data file is kept in the app's private storage and is protected by the operating system's security mechanisms. The app **does not apply additional encryption** to this file. We do not recommend using the app on compromised or rooted devices or on devices without a screen lock.

### 4.2 Answers and letter

Questionnaire answers and the letter text are kept only in memory during the session. They are not written to a database, are not sent to our servers, and are not used for analytics.

### 4.3 System sharing

If you tap **Share**, the letter text (which may include your name) is handed to the operating system's share sheet and to the app or service you choose. This transfer is your action and occurs outside True Love's control. We do not store a copy of what you share. The privacy practices of the app you choose will apply.

## 5. Data collected and shared by third parties

The app uses two third-party services: **Google AdMob** (ads) and **PostHog** (optional analytics).

### 5.1 Google AdMob (ads)

The app displays ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). According to Google's official disclosure for Google Play's Data safety section, the SDK may automatically collect and share:

- **IP address**, which may be used to estimate the device's general location (approximate location);
- **User product interactions**, such as app launch, taps, and video views;
- **Diagnostic information**, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage;
- **Device and account identifiers**, including the Android advertising ID (AAID), the App Set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

This data is used for **advertising and marketing**, **analytics**, and **fraud prevention, security, and compliance**. It may be shared with **Google** and with advertising partners according to the AdMob configuration.

The app **does not request location permission** and does not collect precise location. Approximate location, when present, is inferred by Google from the IP address.

**Personalized ads:** in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, ad personalization depends on your consent, collected through Google's consent platform (UMP). You can review or change these choices on the app's **Privacy** screen when the option is available, or in your device settings. Outside those regions, personalized ads may be shown according to device settings and Google's policies.

**Personalization controls:** you can disable ad personalization in Android settings (for example, "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"). Even without personalization, Google may use data for non-personalized ads, measurement, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.

Learn more:

- Google Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- Google Safety Center: https://safety.google/

Retention of advertising data follows Google's policies and periods, which we do not directly control.

### 5.2 PostHog (optional analytics)

If you enable **"Send anonymous usage data"**, the app sends usage events to **PostHog**, an analytics provider acting as a processor on our behalf.

**Data sent:**

- usage events, such as screens opened, questionnaire started and completed, answers by index and question ID (without the option text), questionnaire duration, letter viewed (only a text-length bucket), sharing, restart, and ad events;
- aggregate ad revenue data, such as placement, currency, value, and precision;
- technical app and device information, such as app version, language, tablet indicator, device model, operating system, and app lifecycle events (install, update, open, and background);
- an **anonymous app identifier**;
- **IP address** and **approximate location** inferred through IP geolocation (GeoIP).

**Data we do not send to PostHog:** your name, the letter text, the text of your answers, your email, your contacts, photos, audio, or files.

**Purposes:** understand the usage funnel, measure retention, diagnose ad failures, and improve the product.

**Where the data is stored:** the PostHog project is configured in the **United States** (`https://us.i.posthog.com`). This involves an international transfer of anonymous usage data to the United States.

**Sharing:** PostHog processes the data on our behalf as a processor. We do not sell this data and do not share it for advertising. PostHog may use subprocessors to provide the service, according to its contract and privacy policy.

**Retention:** we retain analytics events for up to **12 months**, unless a shorter period is configured with the provider.

**How to enable and withdraw:** analytics is **disabled by default**. You can enable it during the first acceptance or later on the app's **Privacy** screen. You can withdraw consent at any time on the same screen. Withdrawal stops new events from being sent. To request deletion of events already sent, use the privacy email listed in this policy.

Learn more:

- PostHog Privacy: https://posthog.com/privacy
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA): https://posthog.com/dpa

### 5.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store on servers:

- your name, the letter text, or the content of your answers;
- your email, phone number, or address;
- your contacts, calendar, photos, videos, audio, or files;
- your precise location;
- health, financial, biometric, or other sensitive data;
- your list of installed apps;
- browsing history.

We do not sell personal data.

## 8. Retention and deletion

- **Local data:** remains on the device until you uninstall the app or clear the app's data. Uninstalling removes the local file.
- **Analytics (PostHog):** up to 12 months, according to the provider's configuration. You can withdraw consent at any time and request deletion of events through the privacy email.
- **Advertising (AdMob):** according to Google's policies and periods. You can reset or delete the advertising ID and disable personalization in Android settings.
- **Sharing:** we do not store copies of what you share through the system menu.

There are no user accounts in True Love. Therefore, there is no "account deletion". To delete local data, uninstall the app or clear the app's data. To delete analytics data, email **jamat3ch@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion — True Love"**. For advertising data, use Google's controls in Android settings and, if you need help, contact us at the same email; where applicable, we will provide guidance and forward the request to the provider. We may request additional information to confirm ownership and prevent improper deletions.

## 9. Security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect data:

- all traffic to AdMob and PostHog is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS;
- the app has no backend, remote database, or proprietary API;
- the local data file is kept in the app's private storage;
- access to third-party services is limited to what is necessary for the purposes described;
- we do not log your name, the letter, or your answers.

No security method is 100% infallible. In the event of a relevant security incident affecting personal data, we will notify data subjects and competent authorities as required by applicable law.
